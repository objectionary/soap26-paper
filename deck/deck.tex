  \pptBanner{Prior Art: \streamliner{} Unrolls Streams into Loops}
  \par
  In 2020, Anders M{\o}ller and Oskar Haarklou Veileborg observed a \emph{60\% slowdown}
  of the stream version on Java~13.
  \par
  They created the first AOT bytecode-to-bytecode stream optimizer.
  \par
  However, objects couldn't escape lambdas, and streams couldn't be
  assigned to variables or passed as arguments.
  \end{pptMiddle}
  }

\plush{
  \begin{pptMiddle}
  \pptBanner{Takeaways}
  \begin{itemize}
  \item On modern JVMs, stream fusion via \ff{mapMulti} matches
    stream unrolling, with \emph{fewer restrictions}.
  \item \(\varphi\)-calculus turns bytecode optimization into
    declarative rewriting: a new optimization is just \emph{new rules}.
  \item Limits so far: only \ff{map}/\ff{filter} are fused;
    closures and functional objects are future work.
  \end{itemize}
  \end{pptMiddle}
